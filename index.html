WHAT WE DO

Multi-Peril Crop Insurance (MPCI)

Federal crop insurance protecting against yield loss from weather, disease, and other natural causes.

  • Revenue Protection (RP)
  • Yield Protection (YP)
  • Area Risk Protection
  • Whole Farm Revenue Protection

Pasture, Rangeland, Forage (PRF)

Rainfall index insurance for pasture and hay acres. No loss adjustment required - payouts based on grid rainfall data.

  • Coverage periods you choose
  • Protects grazing & hay operations
  • Automatic payouts when triggered
  • Subsidized premiums

590 Nutrient Management Plans

NRCS-compliant nutrient management planning by Certified Crop Advisor. Required for many EQIP and state programs.

  • Soil sampling & analysis
  • Manure & fertilizer recommendations
  • Phosphorus Index calculations
  • Annual plan updates
