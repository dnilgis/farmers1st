FFAI WEEKLY // FEBRUARY 19, 2026

The Wind Changed Direction. The Tide Hasn't Turned Yet.

FFAI at 53.6 — the point where cycles either turn or stall. Leading indicators improving. Lagging indicators haven't caught up. Early-stage recovery or a false bottom. The data shows which signals to watch.

S&P is up 100%+ since October '22. Net farm income down 22%. Corn down 48% from highs. Median farm income: negative $1,498 from farming. Government payments $44.3B — highest since 2020 — masking the real picture. Gold nearly tripled to $4,945.

Six Cycle Forces

Dollar / Export Demand BULLISH

DXY down 13% to 98.7. Corn exports raised to record 3.3B bu. India-US deal opens new demand. 10% dollar drop historically precedes 15-20% grain price improvement, 12-18 months.

Gold / Ag Commodities BULLISH 12-24MO

Gold $4,945, silver $85.65. Gap to grain prices widest on record. Gold ATH historically precedes ag commodity rallies 12-24 months out.

Baltic Dry / Trade Flow CONSTRUCTIVE

BDI 2,019, up 135% YoY. Panamax at 1,792. Sustained above 1,800 confirms grain trade acceleration.

Brazil-China / Export Share WATCH

Real 5.33/USD, record 180 MMT Brazil soy. Weakening USD narrows their advantage. Yuan 7.25 = stable Chinese buying power.

WASDE / Price Direction TURNING

Feb WASDE cut corn stocks 100M bu — bullish surprise. 39.2% US in drought, expanding to D3 Extreme. If persists into planting, supply picture changes fast.

Equipment / Profitability BOTTOMING

Deere ATH $593, raised guidance, called 2026 "cycle bottom." Titan Machinery $14.30 still distressed. When TITN turns, sector turn confirmed.

Action Items

1. Hold patience on last 20-30% of 2025 crop

USDA cut stocks, record exports. Dollar weakening. Basis tightening. If no margin calls, data supports holding.

2. Lock input costs now

Crude under $60. Nutrien and Mosaic near 52-week lows. Input score 62.8 is one of the strongest FFAI categories. Lock what you can.

3. Forward contract above $4.70 Dec corn

Layer 20-30% new crop at $4.70+ corn, $12+ Nov beans. Lock margins at today's reduced inputs.

4. Maximize crop insurance — March 15 deadline

ECO/SCO underutilized in Upper Midwest. Call us to verify numbers under new OBBBA provisions.

5. Watch the drought

39% of US in drought. Nebraska D3 Extreme. SWE lowest since 1986. If extends to corn belt, FFAI moves through 60.

6. Don't sell land

Cropland $5,830/ac. Iowa appreciation slowed to 0.7%. Farmer Mac prices diverging from surveys. Better entries possible in 18 months.

7. Government payments are temporary

$44.3B is ad hoc. Debt-to-asset 13.8% historically low. Balance sheet is stronger than income statement. Use that runway.

FFAI METHODOLOGY

48 instruments, 10 weighted categories: Row Crops (20%), Input Costs (15%), Equipment (10%), Grain Trade (8%), Macro (10%), Metals (7%), Livestock (5%), Global Demand (10%), USDA Supply (10%), Farm Structure (5%). Scored 0-100 via proprietary 52-week percentile ranking with momentum and seasonal adjustments. Input costs and select currencies inverse-scored. Forward-looking signal adjustments proprietary.

Sources: CBOT/CME, NYSE/NASDAQ, USDA WASDE, Export Sales, Crop Progress, Drought Monitor, Baltic Exchange, Federal Reserve, BLS. Updated weekly. Published exclusively at farmers1st.com.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is the Farmers First Ag Index?

Proprietary weekly composite index published at farmers1st.com. 48 instruments, 10 categories, scored 0-100. Measures agricultural economic conditions for working farmers. Full methodology and analysis published here every week.

What is the current FFAI score?

53.6 — GUARDED as of February 19, 2026. Leading indicators improving, lagging indicators catching up. Full analysis above.

How do I use the FFAI?

Above 60 = FAVORABLE (lock margins, expand). 45-54 = GUARDED (sell into strength, lock inputs, max insurance). Below 45 = STRESSED (preserve cash, minimize debt). At 53.6: lock inputs, hold grain, maximize insurance.

Crop insurance deadline in Wisconsin?

March 15 for corn, soybeans, spring grains. July 15 acreage reporting. December 1 PRF pasture. Call early — OBBBA changes may affect your options.

What is PRF pasture insurance?

Rainfall index for hay and grazing. Auto payouts below your grid threshold. No adjuster. Heavily subsidized. With 39% of the US in drought as of February 2026 — relevant coverage.

What territory do you cover?
