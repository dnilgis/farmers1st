Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

Who We Are

Farmers First Agri Service is an independent crop insurance agency based in Chetek, Wisconsin. This policy covers farmers1st.com and all associated pages.

What We Collect

Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use our site. GA4 collects anonymized data including pages visited, time on site, device type, and approximate location (city level). Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish users. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

Email subscriptions: If you subscribe to FFAI updates, we collect your email address through Formspree. We use it only to send quarterly index updates and insurance deadline reminders. We do not sell, share, or rent your email to anyone.

Phone and email contact: If you call or email us, we keep records of our conversations as needed for insurance servicing. This is standard practice for licensed insurance agents.

Cookies

This site uses cookies from Google Analytics to measure site traffic. These are first-party cookies set by Google's analytics script. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party advertising services. This site has no ads.

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics 4 — site analytics. Google's Privacy Policy

Formspree — email form handling. Formspree's Privacy Policy

Google Fonts — typeface delivery. Google's Privacy Policy

GitHub Pages — site hosting. GitHub's Privacy Statement

Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months, then automatically deleted. Email subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe. Insurance servicing records are retained as required by state insurance regulations.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your email subscription at any time by replying to any email we send or by contacting us directly. You can opt out of analytics tracking using the tools described above.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
