Crop Insurance

Farmers First Agri Service is an independent crop insurance agency licensed in Wisconsin and Minnesota. All federal crop insurance policies sold through this agency are underwritten by approved insurance providers (AIPs) under a Standard Reinsurance Agreement (SRA) with the Federal Crop Insurance Corporation (FCIC), a division of the U.S. Department of Agriculture Risk Management Agency (USDA RMA).

Crop insurance products, coverage options, premium rates, and program terms are established by USDA RMA and are subject to change. All coverage is subject to the applicable policy provisions, endorsements, and Special Provisions for the county and crop year in question. This website provides general descriptions of available programs and does not constitute a policy, binder, or contract of insurance. Coverage is not effective until a policy is properly applied for, accepted, and confirmed by the approved insurance provider.

Not advice specific to your operation. Information on this site is general in nature. Coverage levels, unit structures, premium rates, APH histories, and program eligibility vary by producer, crop, county, and practice. Contact us directly for guidance specific to your operation before making coverage decisions.

Federal crop insurance is a public-private partnership. Policies may not be available for all crops, practices, or counties. Some products are subject to acreage or revenue limitations. Ask your agent for details applicable to your situation.

Farmers First Ag Index (FFAI)

The Farmers First Ag Index (FFAI) is an agricultural financial conditions index developed and maintained by Farmers First Agri Service. The FFAI uses 16 publicly available data series from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED) to measure U.S. agricultural financial conditions and is internally cross-validated against USDA farm loan delinquency data.

The FFAI has not been reviewed, endorsed, or approved by USDA, RMA, FCIC, the Federal Reserve, or any government agency. Validation is internal and conducted by Farmers First Agri Service. References to statistical validation (r = 0.49, r = 0.51) reflect internal cross-validation results, not third-party or government review.

The model explains approximately 28% of observed variance in farm loan delinquency. The remaining 72% is attributable to factors including weather, trade policy, individual farm management decisions, regional conditions, and crop insurance election choices. The FFAI is a conditions indicator, not a predictive guarantee.

The FFAI is a publicly available resource. Access to FFAI data, API endpoints, and embeddable badges is not contingent on purchasing crop insurance or any other product or service from Farmers First Agri Service. FFAI data is published under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) license — free to use with attribution.

FFAI scores, sub-index values, and historical data are updated quarterly after FRED publishes complete data for the prior quarter. Data displayed on this site may lag real-time market conditions. Publication dates are noted on all current data.
